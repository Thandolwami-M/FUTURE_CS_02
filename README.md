# FUTURE_CS_02
This report presents a real-world phishing example, showing that the email was sent from a fraudulent domain to the  user. This analysis is conducted from the perspective of an analyst, examining real samples to understand how phishing attacks are structured.

Tools used:
- Email header analyser
- Browser tools
- MS Word

Analyser approach:
- The email headers were examined to verify the authenticity of the sender
- The sender's email and domain were analysed to ensure it belongs to a legitimate organization or if it is fradulent.
- The email was reviewed using the header email header analyser, and all red flags from the email are displayed
- Based on the findings that were indicated, they were documented in a structured format.
